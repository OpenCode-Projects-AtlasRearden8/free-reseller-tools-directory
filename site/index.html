Photo Editing

Photopea

A full photo editor that runs in your browser and behaves like Photoshop. Crop, resize, brighten, remove backgrounds, and export for your listings. Files are processed on your own device, so your photos never leave your computer.

Cost: Free with ads on the editor screen; optional ad-free premium subscription

Limits: Free tier shows ads and day-use limits are around 2.5 hours of editing before a short cooldown; premium removes ads

Best pick if you already know Photoshop-style tools. Supports PSD, PNG, JPG, WEBP and more.

photopea.com — verified 2026-11-03

GIMP

The classic free, open-source image editor for Windows, Mac, and Linux. Batch-fix brightness, white balance, crop, and sharpen product shots with no restrictions at all.

Cost: Free forever (open source, donations welcome)

Limits: No account or usage limits; the interface has a learning curve and it is desktop software, not a browser tool

Current stable release: GIMP 3.2.6 (September 2026).

gimp.org — verified 2026-11-03

remove.bg

One-click background removal that cuts your item out and drops it on a clean white background, which is exactly what listing photos want.

Cost: Free previews without an account; a free account gets a small monthly credit of full-resolution downloads

Limits: Free previews come back in low resolution (roughly preview-sized, fine for a quick look but short of full listing quality); paid credits unlock full-resolution and bulk removal

Great for fast, clean cutouts when the free monthly credits cover your volume; otherwise remove backgrounds yourself in Photopea or GIMP.

remove.bg — verified 2026-11-03

Canva

Browser-based design suite with a photo editor, background remover, label templates, and easy resizing. Useful for listing graphics, price tags, and social posts about your sales.

Cost: Free tier; optional Pro subscription

Limits: Free tier limits background remover, some templates, and features to Pro, and limits most free designs to standard export sizes

Lighter editing than Photopea, but faster for polished graphics and print labels.

canva.com — verified 2026-11-03

Keyword Research

EverBee

A Chrome extension that shows real Etsy sales and revenue for any product, plus keyword volume and competition scores. Great for resellers who also make or list on Etsy.

Cost: Free 'Hobby' plan, no credit card required; paid plans add unlimited searches and exports

Limits: Free plan limits keyword searches (roughly ten keyword lookups, tracked in the extension), product-analytics depth, and CSV export

Install the extension and connect an Etsy account to start. Free-plan quotas reset on a monthly cycle.

everbee.com — verified 2026-11-03

Google Trends

Google's free trends explorer. Type a product or brand and see search interest over time, by region, to spot rising demand before it shows up in prices.

Cost: Free, no account needed for basic use

Limits: No exact search-volume numbers (relative index only); fine-grained data gets fuzzy for rarely searched terms

Use 'Explore' to compare two or three product words side by side and check if interest is climbing or fading.

trends.google.com — verified 2026-11-03

AnswerThePublic

Shows the actual questions and phrases people search around a topic, grouped into a visual map. Handy for writing listing titles and descriptions that match what buyers type.

Cost: Free tier tied to daily search limit; paid plans for more

Limits: Free plan caps daily keyword searches (a small number per day) and data updates lag behind paid tiers

One or two targeted searches per research day is plenty at the free tier.

answerthepublic.com — verified 2026-11-03

Shipping Calculators

Pirate Ship

Free shipping software with pre-negotiated USPS Commercial and deeply discounted UPS rates, label printing, batch uploads from a spreadsheet, and shipment tracking. The de facto standard for small resellers.

Cost: Free forever. No monthly fees, no markup, no credit card required; you pay only the postage per label

Limits: Requires a free account to buy labels; US-origin shipments only for the discounted UPS services; paid postage means buying it is a real transaction

Compare USPS vs UPS on the same label screen before printing. Their USPS Rate Calculator and Zone Map are also free without an account.

pirateship.com — verified 2026-11-03

USPS Retail Postage Price Calculator

The Postal Service's own rate calculator: pick destination ZIP or country, weight, and shape, and see retail USPS prices including flat-rate box options.

Cost: Free, no account needed

Limits: Shows USPS retail rates only (no UPS comparison, no commercial rates); formulas change with yearly price updates

Perfect for checking whether a flat-rate Priority box beats a plain first-class or ground option before you print anywhere.

postcalc.usps.com — verified 2026-11-03

Sold-Price Lookups

eBay Advanced Search (Sold Items)

eBay's built-in completed-listings search: check “Sold items” on an ordinary keyword search to see what similar items actually sold for, including shipping shown.

Cost: Free with an eBay account (sign in to see sold listings)

Limits: Sold data only reaches back about 90 days; Best Offer accepted prices can display as the crossed-out asking price instead of the real accepted amount

The source of truth for most comps. Sort by ending-soonest and scan the last several sells, not just the highest.

ebay.com/sch/ebayadvsearch — verified 2026-11-03

PriceCharting

Free price guides for video games, trading cards, comics, Funko Pops, coins, and LEGO sets, with long-range value charts and loose/new/graded price columns.

Cost: Free to browse with a free account; optional premium subscription for advanced tools

Limits: Price accuracy depends on contributors; fewer comps on niche items; premium-only extras include API access and collection reporting

Unmatched free history for retro games and TCG singles; cross-check against eBay sold listings for freshness.

pricecharting.com — verified 2026-11-03

130point

Free sold-price search that aggregates eBay completed sales across major marketplaces and shows the accepted Best Offer price that eBay often hides behind the crossed-out asking price.

Cost: Free, no account required for search; paid collection management is optional

Limits: Mostly trading-card focused; not every eBay sale appears in results; sales history reaches back months, not years; the site blocks some automated access, so check it in your browser

Verified live on 2026-11-03 via the site's own about page and current third-party reviews (direct automated fetches return 403 because the site bot-blocks).

130point.com — verified 2026-11-03

Sales Trackers

Flipwise

An eBay-native inventory and profit tracker. Syncs active and sold listings, computes real profit and ROI after fees and shipping, tracks cost and storage location, and generates tax-ready reports.

Cost: Free plan for up to 24 total listings (new members); paid tiers scale by store size

Limits: Free tier is capped at 24 listings and is meant for trying it out; eBay-centric, other marketplaces are manual entry

Available in the US, Canada, UK, Australia, and much of western Europe.

flipwise.app — verified 2026-11-03

Google Sheets

The zero-cost, old-school answer: a free spreadsheet with template gallery for tracking buys, sales, profit, and storage locations, accessible from any device.

Cost: Free with any Google account, forever

Limits: No automatic marketplace sync; everything is manual entry; big workbooks can get slow

Search 'reseller' or 'inventory' in the template gallery, or see docs/prep-setup.md for a simple one-tab ledger design.

docs.google.com/spreadsheets — verified 2026-11-03

eBay Seller Hub

eBay's built-in selling dashboard: list items, watch sales trends, manage orders, and run basic performance analytics without any third-party tool.

Cost: Free with your eBay account; advanced reporting needs a store subscription

Limits: Designed for eBay only; deeper analytics (profit by item, cost basis, multi-channel) require stores or third-party tools

Start here before paying for anything: supplies, insertion-fee numbers, and basic analytics are all included.

ebay.com/sl/sellerhub — verified 2026-11-03

Label Makers

Avery Design & Print

Free browser design tool for Avery label sheets: templates for address labels, price tags, and product labels, then print on store-bought Avery sheets with any printer.

Cost: Free design tool and templates; you buy the blank label sheets (no minimums, free shipping over $75)

Limits: Designed around Avery sheet sizes (their paper); heavy design features and pre-printed labels are paid

Use the Templates menu to open Design & Print. Works fine for SKU and thank-you labels without a thermal printer.

avery.com — verified 2026-11-03

Labelary

Free online ZPL viewer and barcode generator: paste ZPL or design labels in the browser, preview them, and convert to PNG or PDF. Also generates QR, Code 128, UPC, and more.

Cost: Free web viewer and generator; simple free API tier for the rendering engine

Limits: ZPL-centric (thermal-printer language), so it is most useful if you have a Zebra-style label printer or want to test label code

The barcode generator alone is worth bookmarking for SKU labels.

labelary.com — verified 2026-11-03
